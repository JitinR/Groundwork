# Site coordinate reference acceptance

Updated 4 November 2026. The site workflow accepts manually entered coordinates, measured rectangular/corridor extents and polygon corners. It provides a local concept-model reference, without embedded imagery, survey approval or CRS transformations.

Creating a community or infrastructure site commits its location and fitted extent together. Rectangular corners use the local inverse projection and width-axis angle counterclockwise from east. Polygon center, true area and bounding axes derive from its corners; sorting width/depth also rotates the width axis. Zero coordinates remain valid. Invalid inputs and new extents below one metre in either dimension produce an accessible error before a project update. Polygon corners and shape survive reopening, and unit switches convert dimensions.

The local boundary fill and outline use the same east/north coordinates. Loading saved/shared sites does not request provider imagery. The form and scene disclose that scope. The explicit Google Earth link includes entered coordinates and opens an external service.

Fixtures check an 80 × 60 m rectangle at latitudes 0°, 60° and −60° with rotations 0°, 35° and 90°, including independent great-circle lengths; metre/foot conversion; polygon center/area/axes; atomic saves; serialization; unit changes; invalid inputs; and local fill alignment without image requests. Browser checks use the real site component in a synthetic localhost fixture, without production accounts or projects.

The generated airport now follows its stored runway axis bearing, clockwise from local north (-Z), with east at +X. Runways, taxiways, aprons, terminal and the generated pad rotate together once. Their local metre dimensions are unchanged. Terrain/camera extents use the pad's rotated world bounds. Authored drafts, survey samples, scheduled facilities and MEP remain outside this rotation in their existing coordinate frame. The geographic boundary is not rotated or rewritten. The default and legacy missing-heading value of 90° preserves the east-west layout; an existing non-default stored bearing now affects its generated preview.

Actual scene regressions measure 3,000 m runway length and 45 m width at cardinal and 30° bearings, compare rotated pad bounds with scene extents, and exercise a 30° site-to-runway fit without double rotation. Independent world-coordinate draft, facility and survey fixtures remain in place. These checks cover the rendered schematic and do not establish georeferenced/surveyed infrastructure or aviation design compliance. Legacy schematic PDF symbols remain unscaled and are outside this heading-rendering change.

Existing references are not migrated automatically. Canvas extents retain their existing display-unit rounding while authored boundary coordinates retain separate precision. Infrastructure fitting remains a preliminary heuristic. Survey north/CRS, arbitrary GIS extents, terrain and project engineering require separate acceptance.
